Clan Introduction
WHAT IS

SUZAKU?

Inspired by Suzaku (朱雀)—the mythological Guardian of the South—Dueling Grounds's clan forged for players who fight with precision, agility, and absolute skill. We embody the spirit of rebirth, fierce competition, and relentless strength on the battlefield.

We focus on continuous mechanical improvement, tactical coordination, and high-level gameplay. Whether you are aiming to dominate the leaderboards or grow alongside a dedicated group of warriors, Suzaku provides the ultimate home to refine your craft and rise above. Our core values are Precision, Discipline, Brotherhood, and Resilience.

SPECIALIZED DIVISIONS

ASHIGARU

FIRST STAGE

Ashigaru is the first stage in the development of gameplay in Dueling Grounds at Suzaku. They are players who are in the process of growing and will become the future successors of Suzaku.

BUSHI

SECOND STAGE

Bushi is the second stage after passing the Ashigaru stage. They are players who have mastered basic to intermediate combat skills in Dueling Grounds.

SHOGUN

FINAL STAGE

Shogun is the third and final stage in the Suzaku rank system. They are players with high reputations in Dueling Grounds due to their abilities and serve as the core representatives of the Suzaku clan.
